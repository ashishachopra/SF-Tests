Legend:
  🔥 = HIGH PRIORITY - Execute immediately (critical join bridges)
  ⚡ = MEDIUM PRIORITY - Execute soon (security, ETL, inheritance)
  📊 = LOW PRIORITY - Optional (very small tables)
  ⛔ = NO OPTIMIZATION NEEDED (too small or view)
  ✓ = Key column (PK, FK, or heavily filtered/joined)
  (FK) = Foreign Key
  (PK) = Primary Key

Join Patterns:
  ──► Direct foreign key relationship
  ──┐ Self-referencing relationship
  └─┘ Circular reference
